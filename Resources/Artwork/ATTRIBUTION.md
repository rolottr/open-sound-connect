# Artwork

`AppIconSource.png` was generated for this project on 2026-10-14: a graphite tile with a gold over-ear headphone emblem. It contains no Sony logo, wordmark or product photograph and is provided under the repository's MIT license.

The `SoundConnectArtwork` build tool masks the source tile, adds a shadow and renders the macOS icon sizes. The WH-1000XM4, WH-1000XM5 and WH-1000XM6 models in `Resources/Models` and their rendered thumbnails were authored for this project in Blender. Editable geometry, materials and the generators are in `artwork/`; no product photographs, third-party models or font files are bundled (the XM5/XM6 earcup outline is a numeric fit traced from a product photograph, which is not included). The models are visual approximations, not manufacturer CAD. Other headphone models use the generic SwiftUI illustration in `HeroView.swift`. Sony names and marks identify the depicted product; trademark rights are not granted by the project's MIT license.
